Alameda County Sheriff's Office arrest records
What this is — and isn't. Arrest records published by the Alameda County Sheriff's Office (unincorporated communities such as Castro Valley, Ashland, Cherryland and San Lorenzo, contract cities such as Dublin, plus county facilities — jails, courts — inside other cities). It does not cover cities with their own police departments: the City of Alameda (Alameda PD) is not in this dataset beyond a handful of incidental records. An arrest is an allegation, not a conviction. This is not an official record of any agency.

Method & provenance

  • Source: Crime_Reports_Jul2022_Present.geojson, ACSO crime reports layer, downloaded from the Alameda County Open Data Hub on 2026-08-15. The verbatim file is committed in the repository (data/source/); every figure here derives from it.
  • Record grain: one row per arrest charge; every source row carries an arrest ID (this is an arrest dataset, whatever the layer's name says). Exact-duplicate rows (same arrest, charge and timestamp) were removed — 2 in the current build.
  • Categories are the NIBRS-style label the Sheriff's Office appends to each charge description; leading numeric codes are stripped for display. Rows tagged LOCAL WARRANT / OUT WARRANT are shown as “Warrant service”. About 2,000 rows carry no category at all — including ~1,300 with an empty charge description, shown by statute code only — and appear as “Uncategorized”. One statute assist is applied: dash-less VC 23152/23153 rows (driving under the influence) are counted in the DUI category; no other recategorization is done.
  • Community names are normalized for obvious truncations in the export (OA → Oakland, CAS → Castro Valley, HA → Hayward, LI → Livermore, ALCO → Alameda County). Note that arrests recorded in Oakland, San Leandro or Hayward are largely at county facilities or on the unincorporated fringe — they say nothing about those cities' own crime rates, which their own police departments report separately.
  • Privacy: the source includes an exact street address per record; this site drops that field and shows the block-level location only, with coordinates rounded to ≈11 m. No names or booking photos appear anywhere in the source or here.
  • Timestamps are shown as published (the export stamps them UTC; the county does not document the timezone). Monthly averages exclude the two partial months.
  • Honest gaps: filters, tiles, charts and the table all render the same slice; empty means empty, and partial months are marked, never smoothed.
